## Notes

- Fixtures are in-memory Canonical Artifacts, not 100k filesystem entries.
- `Marshal` / `Encode` validate the document before writing, so serialize figures include reconstruction and fingerprint self-check.
- Decode allocates more because strict duplicate-key walking and field presence
  checks parse tokens before reconstructing the artifact.
- No flaky wall-clock thresholds are asserted in CI.
